About Me

Jesus is our Lord and God, and He loves you!

Hi, I’m AQUIL the eagle. I’m a creative person with a vivid imagination, and my Christian Orthodox faith is an important part of my life. I care about creativity, kindness, and treating people with understanding. This page is a little introduction to who I am, what I enjoy, and some of the things that are important to me.

My interests

I’m passionate about computer science, traditional and semi-digital art, and music composition. They let me explore ideas in different ways: computer science through technology and problem-solving, art through images, and composition through sound. I enjoy the process of imagining something and finding a way to express it, whether I’m drawing, working digitally, or creating music.

Minecraft is another interest of mine. I enjoy it as a place to play, explore, and build. I’m also interested in other things that inspire my curiosity and imagination.

Faith and values

My Orthodox Christian faith is central to who I am. I believe that Jesus is our Lord and God, and that He loves you. My faith gives me hope and influences how I try to approach life and treat other people—with kindness, patience, and understanding.

I try to be considerate of others and to understand that everyone has their own experiences. I also believe respect should go both ways. I want to treat people well, while being clear about my boundaries if I am not treated with respect.

How I experience the world

I’m autistic—specifically, I have Asperger’s syndrome. I also experience emotional difficulties. Writing, speaking, and retaining information can be challenging for me, so expressing an idea or remembering details may sometimes take extra effort. These challenges are part of my experience, but they do not describe all of who I am.

I’m still a person with interests, faith, creativity, and my own way of seeing the world. I try to be patient with myself when something is difficult, and I appreciate kindness and understanding from others.

I don’t give up easily. There are difficult moments when I may need to start again, but I keep trying and take things one step at a time. Starting over does not mean I have failed; it can be a way to continue when things have become especially hard.

Beloved Bible verses

These are some Bible verses that are meaningful to me:

  • Isaiah 40:31 — a reminder of hope and renewed strength.
  • Luke 15:7 — a reminder of the joy of repentance.
  • 1 John 1:9 — a reminder of forgiveness and God’s faithfulness.
